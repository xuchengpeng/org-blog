RSS 和 Atom Feed 订阅格式

Nov 19, 2026 #Emacs #RSS
Table of Contents
  • RSS 2.0 格式
  • Atom 1.0 格式
  • Feed 生成

RSS 与 Atom 是最常见的两种基于 XML 的 Feed 订阅格式，用于向阅读器或聚合器推送网站的最新更新。两者核心目的相同，但标签结构、规范严谨程度和时间格式有所差异。

RSS 2.0 格式

RSS 2.0 结构简单直观，顶层为 <rss version="2.0"> ，频道信息放在 <channel> 内，每篇文章对应一个 <item> ，时间采用 RFC 822 格式。 

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<rss version="2.0">
  <channel>
    <title>示例博客</title>
    <link>https://example.com</link>
    <description>分享技术与生活笔记</description>
    <language>zh-CN</language>
    <lastBuildDate>Thu, 19 Nov 2026 12:00:00 +0800</lastBuildDate>

    <item>
      <title>第一篇文章标题</title>
      <link>https://example.composts/1</link>
      <guid isPermaLink="true">https://example.composts/1</guid>
      <pubDate>Thu, 19 Nov 2026 10:00:00 +0800</pubDate>
      <author>author@example.com (张三)</author>
      <description><![CDATA[<p>这是文章摘要或正文内容。</p>]]></description>
    </item>
  </channel>
</rss>

Atom 1.0 格式

Atom 由 IETF 制定为正式标准，规范更严格，自带命名空间（ xmlns="http://w3.org" ），根节点为 <feed> ，每篇文章对应一个 <entry> ，时间采用 RFC 3339（ISO 8601 子集）格式。 

<?xml version="1.0" encoding="UTF-8"?>
<feed xmlns="http://w3.org">
  <title>示例博客</title>
  <subtitle>分享技术与生活笔记</subtitle>
  <link href="https://example.com" rel="self"/>
  <link href="https://example.com"/>
  <id>https://example.com</id>
  <updated>2026-11-19T12:00:00+08:00</updated>

  <entry>
    <title>第一篇文章标题</title>
    <link href="https://example.composts/1"/>
    <id>urn:uuid:123e4567-e89b-12d3-a456-426614174000</id>
    <published>2026-11-19T10:00:00+08:00</published>
    <updated>2026-11-19T10:30:00+08:00</updated>
    <author>
      <name>张三</name>
      <email>author@example.com</email>
    </author>
    <summary>这是文章摘要。</summary>
    <content type="html"><![CDATA[<p>这是文章完整内容。</p>]]></content>
  </entry>
</feed>

Feed 生成

之前的文章也介绍了如何为博客生成RSS ，其核心是解析已导出的 HTML 文件，再生成最终所需要的 XML 文件。

当前博客在导出 Org 文件为 HTML 文件前， 在 :preparation-function 中已解析了所有的文章相关的信息1，可以直接在这里生成 XML 文件，以下给出示例代码。 

(defvar +org-blog-site-url "https://www.example.com"
  "Absolute URL of the published site, without trailing slash.")

(defvar +org-blog-title "Chuck's Blog"
  "Site title used in the Atom feed.")

(defun +org-blog-generate-atom-feed (posts base-dir)
  "Generate an Atom 1.0 feed from POSTS into BASE-DIR.

POSTS is the post plist list built in `+org-blog-pages-prepare',
sorted from oldest to newest.  Write `atom.xml' with the 20 most
recent posts and return its file name."
  (let* ((entries (reverse (seq-take (reverse posts) 20))) ; newest first
         (feed-updated (plist-get (car entries) :date)))
    (with-temp-file (expand-file-name "atom.xml" base-dir)
      (insert "<?xml version=\"1.0\" encoding=\"utf-8\"?>\n"
              "<feed xmlns=\"http://www.w3.org/2005/Atom\">\n"
              (format "  <title>%s</title>\n" (xml-escape-string +org-blog-title))
              (format "  <id>%s/</id>\n" +org-blog-site-url)
              (when feed-updated
                (format "  <updated>%s</updated>\n" (format-time-string "%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ" feed-updated t)))
              (format "  <link href=\"%s/\" rel=\"alternate\" type=\"text/html\"/>\n" +org-blog-site-url)
              (format "  <link href=\"%s/atom.xml\" rel=\"self\" type=\"application/atom+xml\"/>\n" +org-blog-site-url)
              "  <author><name>Chuck</name></author>\n")
      (dolist (post entries)
        (let ((link (concat +org-blog-site-url "/"
                            (file-name-sans-extension
                             (file-relative-name (plist-get post :file) base-dir))
                            ".html")))
          (insert "  <entry>\n"
                  (format "    <title>%s</title>\n" (xml-escape-string (plist-get post :title)))
                  (format "    <id>%s</id>\n" link)
                  (format "    <link href=\"%s\" rel=\"alternate\" type=\"text/html\"/>\n" link)
                  (format "    <published>%s</published>\n" (format-time-string "%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ" (plist-get post :date) t))
                  (format "    <updated>%s</updated>\n" (format-time-string "%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%SZ" (plist-get post :date) t))
                  (dolist (tag (plist-get post :tags))
                    (format "    <category term=\"%s\"/>\n" (xml-escape-string tag)))
                  (when (org-string-nw-p (plist-get post :description))
                    (format "    <summary>%s</summary>\n" (xml-escape-string (plist-get post :description))))
                  "  </entry>\n")))
      (insert "</feed>\n"))
    (expand-file-name "atom.xml" base-dir)))

1
